The goals

= four things the AI should do well, for anyone who speaks Esan or wants to learn it.

Goal 1 of 4

Hear it

Speech recognition that turns spoken Esan into accurate text.

Goal 2 of 4

Speak it

Dialect-aware text-to-speech with the real rise and fall of Esan tone.

Goal 3 of 4

Translate it

Translation between Esan and English, in both directions.

Goal 4 of 4

Teach it

A patient tutor that helps a new generation learn to speak Esan.

And in time: a living archive of Esan voices, and a voice assistant that answers in Esan.

One language, many voices

In Esan, tone carries meaning: the same syllables, pitched differently, say different things.

Uromi Ekpoma Irrua Ubiaja Ugboha Igueben Ewohimi Ewu

Every town has its own melody, so the AI has to learn them all.

The progress

= where the build stands today, step by step.

  1. Plan and open repo

    Five-layer architecture planned; goals and code open on GitHub.

    Done

  2. Prototype tools built

    Spelling normaliser, syllable and tone tiers, IPA converter, dialect registry.

    Done

  3. First word list sent out

    About 70 Esan words and phrases, out to native speakers for recording.

    Done

  4. First recordings

    Native speakers are recording the word list: the first seeds of the speech dataset.

    Underway

  5. esantop, the Esan AI terminal

    Screens for the monitor, tone QA and dialects are drawn on GitHub; the build is next.

    Designing

  6. Pilot dialect dataset

    Four speakers from one dialect, about 10 hours of tone-marked sentences.

    Next

  7. First models that hear and speak

    A speech-recognition model, then the first Esan voice, scored by native speakers.

    Next

The plan is drawn, the code is open, and the first Esan voices are being recorded.

Open source · design stage

esantop

= the Esan AI terminal: one screen to watch the work, from words coming in to models in training, split by dialect.

  • Monitor: corpus hours per dialect, tone coverage, speakers, training jobs
  • Tone QA: flagged clips go to a native speaker, never auto-corrected
  • Dialects: every dialect in the registry, side by side
